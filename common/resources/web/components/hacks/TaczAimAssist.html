Auto Detect Passable Blocks
Reads what stops bullets from the game itself, mirroring TaCZ's own bullet ray-trace: blocks in the synced tacz:bullet_ignore tag and blocks without a collision shape (tall grass, flowers, ferns, snow layers...) never stop bullets and are treated as see-through automatically - no manual entries needed. Sync Shoot Origin In The Air
TaCZ spawns bullets from the position the gun was drawn at, and only refreshes that origin when a gun is drawn. After flying, falling or travelling far the bullets keep spawning from the old spot, so every shot misses no matter how well you aim. With this option on, the module re-sends a draw to the server when you have moved more than 2 blocks from the last synced position: in the air always, and on the ground when a target-type entity is visible (an engagement is likely), so the origin catches up before the first shot of a fight.

Each sync costs the gun's put-away time (about 0.43s for the default SCAR-L) before the next shot goes through, so syncing happens while you reposition - at most once per 1.5 seconds - and never while you are holding fire. Aiming, visibility checks and the drawn trajectory are always computed from the real bullet origin, so even between syncs the aim stays where the bullets go.
Auto Fire
Fires automatically when the target lock holds a verified firing solution: target visible, a ballistic solution found (when gun data is available), and a TaCZ gun in the main hand. Shots go through the TaCZ client shoot controllers, so the gun's own cooldown and fire mode routing apply; an empty magazine still auto-reloads. Firing pauses automatically while a screen is open or the window loses focus.
Follow Gun Fire Mode
Full-auto guns (and continuous-burst guns) keep firing while the target is engaged; semi-auto and short-burst guns fire one shot per newly acquired target.
Single Shot Per Target
One shot the moment a new enemy is acquired. Re-fires when the same target is lost and reacquired, or when the lock switches to another enemy.
Full Auto
Keeps firing for as long as the target lock holds a firing solution, regardless of the gun's own fire mode.
Teleport Repositioning
Searches around you for a standable spot with line of sight to an enemy that is fully hidden behind cover, and teleports there. The column scan reaches up to 20 blocks above your feet, and elevated spots that stand above the enemy's eye line at a healthy distance (high ground shooting over walls) are strongly preferred; any sight-line spot is the fallback. The spot is drawn at random and the previously attempted one is avoided, so repeated failures never keep hitting the same position. The automatic trigger needs a TaCZ gun in hand; at most one teleport per 3 seconds. Once the enemy becomes visible from the new spot, the target lock acquires it and Auto Fire takes over.
Automatic - Hidden Enemy
Teleports on its own when the closest hidden enemy stays fully hidden for half a second and you have nothing visible to engage.
Manual - Script Only
No automatic teleports. Trigger the search from your own script, for example bound to a key: Returns true when a teleport was performed.

Automatic Bullet Calibration

Reads installed TaCZ gun packs from the game's tacz folder and from mods jars (TaCZ ships its default gun pack inside its own mod jar). Local data takes priority over learned data.
