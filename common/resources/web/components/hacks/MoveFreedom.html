Cancels TaCZ medical movement penalties locally: fracture slows you down per injury layer, and carrying too much slows you down by up to 90%. With the matching switch on, you move at full speed while the status effect icons still show as usual.
The penalties are cancelled only for your own movement simulation - nothing is sent to the server. Turning a switch off takes effect on the next tick, the game re-applies the penalty by itself.
