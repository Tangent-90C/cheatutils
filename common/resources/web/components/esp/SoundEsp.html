Marks the position of every sound listed below with Entity-ESP style boxes and tracers. Sounds that are not in the list are never marked, and every rule that is in effect is a row here - a pattern such as minecraft:counterstrike.weapons.* stands for every sound with that prefix, and a longer pattern wins over a shorter one (that is how weapons.fx.* separates bullet impacts from gunfire). Each row has its own settings: whether to draw the marker and its tracer, through walls, only while the position is blocked, the shape, how far the marker is moved down (gunshots play at the shooter's head, so 1.6 puts the box back around the player), and its own color, which overrides the one derived from the sound.
